Team EchoMind

EchoMind was built with a singular vision: turning the noise of endless meetings into clear, searchable, and actionable intelligence.

Our Mission

To build the most accessible, privacy-centric workspace intelligence platform. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence, we empower professionals to unlock the hidden value in their everyday conversations without sacrificing data sovereignty.

Our Vision

A future where no meeting note is lost, no action item forgotten, and every professional can effortlessly recall any detail from past projects using semantic search instead of manual note-taking.
